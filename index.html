Frequently asked questions

What happens on the free intro call?

We talk for 20 minutes about your goal and where you're stuck. You leave with a clear next step, whether or not we work together.

How are sessions held?

Online by video call or phone. In-person sessions can be arranged on request.

How long until I see results?

Most people notice more clarity after the first two sessions and visible progress within 6 to 8 weeks.

Can I reschedule a session?

Yes. Tell us at least 24 hours before the session and we'll move it for free.

How do I pay?

Choose your program in the Pay online section. You can pay by card, UPI or net banking through our secure payment provider.

What is your refund policy?

If you cancel at least 48 hours before your first session, you get a full refund. After that, unused sessions can be rescheduled.
